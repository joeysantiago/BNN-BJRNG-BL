I turn business needs into software that holds up.

I like owning a feature all the way down: the table and the query, the service that applies the business rules, the API between client and server, and the Angular screen at the end. Knowing the whole path means that when something breaks or slows down, I can follow it to the cause instead of passing it to someone else.

The domains have changed from job to job: drug testing, document signing, payroll, recruitment, corporate learning and accounting. The common thread is software where a wrong value has consequences, such as a lab result, a salary or a signed document, so I treat validation and edge cases as part of the feature.

I hold an MCA from NIT Raipur, one of India's National Institutes of Technology, and a B.Sc. in Physics. Outside work I explore IoT, which keeps me curious about the hardware end of software.
